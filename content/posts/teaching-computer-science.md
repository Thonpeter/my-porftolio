Teaching computer science taught me more about software engineering than any single technical manual. When explaining recursion, memory allocation, or network protocols to a beginner, you cannot hide behind buzzwords or hand-waving abstractions.

## First Principles over Memorization

Students often struggle when taught syntax before mental models. Once they visualize how data structures live in memory or how HTTP requests traverse client and server:

- The syntax becomes secondary.
- Debugging becomes an active deduction process rather than trial-and-error.

Effective teaching and effective engineering share the same goal: making the complex understandable and dependable.
